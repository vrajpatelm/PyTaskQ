# Code of conduct

PyTaskQ follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't accepted in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (vrajpatelm). Reports are
handled confidentially, and the maintainer may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who
breaks these rules.
